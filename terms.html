The Service

Wopezi translates text, camera captures, photos, live speech, video audio and documents on your device, and lets you save translated outputs as TXT, SRT, PDF, natural-voice M4A audio, subtitled or dubbed MP4 video, or layout-preserving DOCX / PPTX / PDF documents. Voices come from the speech engine built into iOS.
