Wopezi is operated by Wopezi ("we", "us"). We don't run servers that receive your content, and we never sell or share personal data.
01

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Translation projects — typed text, recognized text (OCR), speech transcripts, translations and your edits.
  • Source media — working copies of the photos, videos, recordings and documents you choose. Originals in Photos or Files are never modified.
  • Saved outputs — the TXT, SRT, MP4 or PDF files you decide to save.
  • Credit ledger — your local credit balance, spend records and verified purchase identifiers.
  • Preferences — language pair, default format, theme and session state.

All of this lives in the app's private storage until you delete it.

02

Permissions

  • CameraON TAP
    Only when you tap Capture Text in Camera Scan, to photograph printed text.
  • PhotosON TAP
    Only when you choose a photo or video to translate or subtitle.
  • Microphone & Speech RecognitionON TAP
    Only when you tap Record Speech, or when a chosen video's audio is turned into subtitles. Apple's on-device speech services are used where available.
  • NotificationsOPTIONAL
    Lets you know when an export finishes while Wopezi is in the background.
✅
You can change any permission in iOS Settings. Wopezi does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.
03

How Processing Works

Translation, text recognition, subtitle generation and document export run on your device using bundled language packs and Apple system frameworks. Wopezi does not send your content to third-party AI or translation services.

04

In-App Purchases

Credit packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details. Wopezi stores the transaction ID, product and granted amount locally to keep your balance accurate.

05

Log Out, Delete Account & Erase All App Data

  • Log Out ends the local session. Projects, outputs and credits stay on the device.
  • Delete Account and Erase All App Data permanently remove all local projects, media, outputs, preferences and your remaining credit balance.
⚠️
Deletion can't be undone, and remaining credits are lost.
06

Children

Wopezi is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
